Conferences & Schools

A selection of international conferences, workshops, and summer schools I have attended, focusing on quantum computing, machine learning, and their intersections. These events provided opportunities to learn from leading experts, present research, and foster collaborations within the scientific community.

  • Joint ICTP-WE Heraeus School and Conference on Frontiers at the Intersection of Quantum Simulation and Machine Learning, Trieste, Italy.

    Attended a two-week program combining a school and conference on the interplay between quantum simulation and machine learning. The event gathered leading experts and researchers to foster collaboration across the two fields. Core topics included quantum simulation of many-body systems, data-driven characterization of quantum systems, quantum state representation with ML models, and protocol optimization for quantum control.

    • Learn more

  • Topics in Modern Machine Learning DIBRIS, Univiersity of Genoa, Genoa.

    The program included a boot camp on statistical learning, ML models, and optimization, followed by lectures and practical sessions on cutting-edge topics such as implicit regularization, sketching, reinforcement learning, inverse problems, optimal transport, and fairness in ML. The school concluded with a workshop featuring invited speakers.

    • Learn more

  • Quantum Information Theory 2023 ICMAT, Autonomous University of Madrid, Madrid, Spain.

    Attended a two-months research program focused on the mathematical foundations of quantum information. The program combined two advanced schools and three thematic focus weeks on topics including optimization methods, tensor networks, quantum many-body systems, functional analysis, and operator algebras. Engaged in lectures, specialized workshops, and collaborative discussions with leading researchers in the field.
